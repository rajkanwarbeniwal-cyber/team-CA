# Sign Up & Login - Quick Guide

## ✅ Status: WORKING NOW

Your authentication is now fully functional with **password-based signup and login**.

---

## 📝 How to Sign Up

### Step 1: Go to Sign-Up Page
```
https://taksha.education/auth/sign-up
```

### Step 2: Fill Form
```
Full Name:     Your Name
Email:         your@email.com
Password:      At least 6 characters
Confirm Pass:  Same as above
```

### Step 3: Click "Create Account"
- Account created immediately ✅
- Logged in automatically
- Redirected to dashboard

**No email verification needed** - Account works immediately!

---

## 🔐 How to Login

### Step 1: Go to Login Page
```
https://taksha.education/auth/sign-in
```

### Step 2: Select "Password" Tab
(You'll see OTP and Password tabs at the top)

### Step 3: Enter Credentials
```
Email:    Your registered email
Password: Your password
```

### Step 4: Click "Sign In"
- Logged in immediately ✅
- Redirected to dashboard
